### 3. Netflix Movies & TV Shows Exploratory Data Analysis

#### Objective

Conducted exploratory data analysis to identify trends and patterns within Netflix's movies and TV shows catalog.

#### Tech Stack

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

#### Process

* Cleaned and transformed raw Netflix dataset.
* Performed univariate and bivariate analysis.
* Analyzed content by genre, rating, release year, duration, and country.
* Built visualizations to communicate findings effectively.
* Derived insights to support content strategy and audience understanding.

#### Key Insights

* Movies represent a larger portion of Netflix's catalog compared to TV shows.
* Content additions increased substantially after 2015.
* Certain genres consistently dominate the platform's content library.
* Content availability varies significantly across countries.
* TV-MA and TV-14 are among the most frequently assigned ratings.
